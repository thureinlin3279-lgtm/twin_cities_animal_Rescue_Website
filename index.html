Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming abandoned and neglected animals throughout the Minneapolis–Saint Paul metro area. We believe every animal deserves a safe, loving home, and we work tirelessly to connect our community with pets in need.

Our Impact

Since our founding, we have rescued over 3,200 animals from overcrowded shelters and unsafe situations. More than 2,800 pets have found permanent homes through our adoption program. Our dedicated network of 450 active volunteers contributes over 18,000 hours of service each year. We partner with 27 local veterinary clinics to provide low-cost spay and neuter services to the community.

How You Can Help

  • Adopt: Give a rescue animal a forever home and free up space for another pet in need.
  • Foster: Provide temporary care for animals while they wait for their permanent families.
  • Volunteer: Help at events, transport animals, or assist with daily shelter operations.
  • Donate: Your financial support covers veterinary care, food, and supplies.
