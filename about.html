Our Story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue was founded in 2009 by a group of local veterinarians and animal lovers who saw an urgent need for a community-based rescue organization. What started as a small network of foster homes has grown into a full-service rescue operation serving the entire Minneapolis–Saint Paul metro area.

Core Values

  • Compassion: We treat every animal with kindness, dignity, and respect.
  • Community: We believe in the power of neighbors helping neighbors.
  • Transparency: We are open about our operations, finances, and outcomes.
  • Commitment: We never give up on an animal who can be saved.
